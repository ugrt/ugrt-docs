## Getting started

The electrical subsection holds meetings on a weekly basis every Wednesday at 7:00 PM. Electrical holds meetings at the same time as firmware.

## Tools that we use

Electrical mainly uses [[/electrical/KiCad]] to design schematics and printed circuit boards. We use KiCad version `9.0`.
