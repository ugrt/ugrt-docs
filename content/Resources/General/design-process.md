## Constraints

Figure out what constraints your project has. This includes mechanical fit, board area, connector placement, power budget, thermal limits, and how easy the connectors are to reach. All of these affect the design before you draw a schematic.

## Power and Thermal Management

A good rule of thumb is 0.2 to 0.3mm for signals and 1mm for power traces up to 2 Amps. See [[/Resources/Electrical Resources/can-harness|CAN Wiring Harness]] for harness sizing and [[/Archive/Electrical-Folder-25-26/distro-board|the PDB]] for how power is distributed.
