## Getting started

The electrical subsection holds meetings on a weekly basis every Wednesday at 7:00 PM in `RICH 1529`. Electrical holds meetings at the same time as firmware. See the electrical [Gallery](#gallery) for a preview of the kind of work Electrical does.

To get started check out [[/Resources/Electrical Resources/kicad | KiCad and Footprints]].

## Tools that we use

Electrical uses [[/Resources/Electrical Resources/kicad | KiCad]] (version `9.0`) to design the 2025/2026 schematics and printed circuit boards. The team has since moved to [[/Resources/Electrical Resources/altium | Altium]]. See [[/Resources/home|Resources]] for the full set of guides, including [[/Resources/Electrical Resources/can|CAN]] and the [[/Resources/Electrical Resources/can-harness|CAN wiring harness]] standards.
